The panel sections by `group` (`SOUS_GROUPS`: what KIND of setting it is), so a check's switch

looking live. The same panel is mounted in Settings and inside the Findings panel.

## The change

1. **One section per check, in `SOUS_SETTINGS` order.** The check's own switch is the section's
   header toggle; its sub-switches and thresholds sit under it and are disabled while the check
   is off. `general` settings come last in their own section ("Shared by several checks"), which
   is where `word_support_floor` now lives (it tunes Casing, WordLength and LetterRun).
2. **Check names and one-line descriptions are Sefer's**, a small table keyed by `SousCheck`
   (`Casing` → "Capital letters", `Doubled` → "Repeated words"…), in `src/app/sousSettings.ts`
   beside the existing labels. A check kitchen adds later shows its id until it gets a row.
3. **Each section gets an anchor**, `data-sous-check={check}`, and the Settings route accepts
   `?check=<SousCheck>`: it scrolls to that section and highlights it briefly.
4. **A finding links to its check.** Wherever a Sous finding shows (the Findings panel row, the
   inline popover), a **Tune this check** action goes to `?check=${checkOf(code)}`. No action
   when `checkOf` is `null` (hygiene: a control character is a defect, not a threshold).
   `code` is Sefer's `sous.<lane>[.<class>]`; strip the `sous.` prefix before `checkOf`.
5. `SOUS_GROUPS` and `group` stay only as a small label on a row (switch / count / share) if
   they still help; otherwise they go.
