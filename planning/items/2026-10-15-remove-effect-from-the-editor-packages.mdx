**Done 2026-11-02** (branch `chore/kitchen-from-nexus`): `@wycliffeassociates/sefer-text/result` is a plain `Result` with Effect's field names, the three errors are `Error` subclasses, the app checks these results with that module's guards and lifts them with `#core/result`'s `fromResult`, and `pnpm boundaries` refuses `effect` in either package. The reason to do it now: the packages are to be published (dashboard-2), and an rc-pinned peer would hold every installing app to Sefer's Effect. The packages were renamed from `@sefer/*` in the same change.

For purity, `@wycliffeassociates/sefer-text` and `@wycliffeassociates/sefer-editor` could stop depending on Effect.
Today it is a peer dependency of both (2026-10-15).

> Fine for nwo and document in ideas might should remove for purity sake

## Why it stays for now

- **The hub already has it.** It depends on `effect@4.0.0-rc.112`, the same
  version as Sefer, in both `web/` and `spikes/viewer`. So the peer
  dependency costs the hub nothing.
- **The packages use little of it:**
  - `Result` in the Book port (`apply` returns `Result<Receipt, Refusal>`)
  - `Data.TaggedError` for `Refusal`, `SourceDecodeError` and
    `SourceChangeError`
  - `Result` from `decode`
- **The app depends on those being Effect's.** About 20 `apply` sites check
  the result with Effect's `Result.isFailure` / `isSuccess`, and 15 files use
  `Refusal`. Effect's helpers look only at `_tag`, but its `Result` type,
  `isResult` and `yield*` need Effect's own prototype. So plain look-alikes
  would not typecheck at those sites.

## What removing it would take

- The packages define their own small `Result` and error classes.
- Sefer's core adapts them at the boundary, in one module that turns a package
  `Result` into Effect's.
- Every `apply` / `decode` call site goes through that adapter.

It is mechanical, but it touches about 35 files.
