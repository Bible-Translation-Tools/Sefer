**Folded into [form mode, verse per line, and the editor's sharp edges](/items/2026-10-14-form-mode)**
(2026-10-20). What was fixed here:

- **The headline rule** is at the top of `documentation/architecture/editor.md`: a CodeMirror line
  is not a USFM line.
- **Backspace in an open footnote** deleted the whole note (f10cfb5). An open note was one
  point-painted set, so a deletion in its body widened to the note. The body is now an editable
  span when `note.body` is directly mutable. Not a regression from the package move.
- **The corresponding block** was unpaired on Bisa Mark beside Cebuano (ebe13ad): the two texts
  wrote the book code in different cases (`\id mrk` and `MRK`). Sids now compare case-blind.
  `data-pair` on the reference pane's root still names a failure: `unpaired:<sid>`,
  `no-source` or `no-caret`.
