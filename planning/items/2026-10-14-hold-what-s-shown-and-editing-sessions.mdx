**Parked 2026-10-20.** Will: probably a misunderstanding of what was already built, and may not need doing.

**Decided direction (Oct 7): canonicalise, no new primitive.** A view keeps what it showed until the
reader moves on, and marks a row settled or stale rather than removing it. The behaviour already
exists in pieces, and is sometimes jumpy:

- **Review:** the settled card keeps the diff on screen after the sides stop differing (`ReviewPanel.tsx`, `settled()`).
- **Findings:** stale rows carry a badge instead of vanishing (`hideStale`, `src/core/findings/filter.ts`).
- **The editor:** a finding fixed mid-keystroke drops its decoration and the text jumps. It should hold, marked fixed, until the cursor leaves the tile.

<Followup id="one-rule">Write it down as one rule with one helper when the next caller arrives, probably key-term approvals, or when one of the three next gets jumpy.</Followup>

## Editing sessions

The designer's PR #5 (merged) added "Approve" and "Approve with edits" to key terms with an editing
session per card. Two places want a per-card edit: key terms ("approved with edits" means changed
since the card opened) and Review (take a side, then fix a punctuation mark). Undo history is
global today: per-card undo would light up undo buttons on every other card of the same book.

**Approve with edits needs no session:** it can mean "the verse differs from the saved file when you
approve", the disk-vs-working baseline Review already uses.

**Built since (2026-10-15):** a card can hold its own session. `mountCard({ session: true })`
keeps the inverse of that card's edits, so Key terms' Cancel undoes only what was typed in the
card, never someone else's edit (`packages/editor/src/recipes/card.ts`). Undo history itself is
still global.
