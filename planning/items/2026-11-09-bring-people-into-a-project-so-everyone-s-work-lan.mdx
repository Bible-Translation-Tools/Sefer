For paisano: Thomas has a Jira note to come back to this. Drafted 2026-11-09 from the save and
sync states conversation (SEFER-92, SEFER-83). Nothing is built; today Sefer has no team,
collaborator or invite code, and permissions can only be changed on the WACS website.

The core problem:

> A team working on one project should end up with one shared project, without anyone having
> to understand repositories, permissions or forks.

Today someone who downloads a project they may not write to has their work sent, silently, to
their own copy on first send. A team can end up with several copies someone must combine by hand.

## Stories

> As the person responsible for a translation project, I want to easily bring my team members
> into the project, so that everything they send goes straight into our shared project and I
> don't have to find and combine work scattered across separate copies.

> As a translator joining a project, I want to know that I've been added and that my work is
> going to the team's project, so that I can work without worrying whether my changes actually
> reached everyone else.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The owner can add a person to the project without needing to understand WACS repositories or
   permission settings.
2. The added person can then send directly to the shared project; no personal copy is created.
3. The owner can see who is on the project and remove someone.
4. Someone who may not send to the shared project is told before they start, or at the latest at
   their first send, in plain words.
5. When someone who already has their own copy is added later, Sefer offers to send that work to
   the shared project.
6. Only people who may manage the project see the option to add others.

The smallest version worth shipping: 1, 2 and 4.

Out of scope for now: an invite link (Gitea has no "anyone with this link" feature), inviting by
email someone without a WACS account, creating organisations or teams.
