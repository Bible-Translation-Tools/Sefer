- **The footnote editor bypassed every rule** (it was trusted). Its edits are now judged by the Book's own rules under the note's projection: a typed `\`, or a paste with `\c 9`, is refused; typing into a fresh empty note works. `dac8ddf`

Policy is `LEGACY_MARKERS` in `src/app/legacyMarkers.ts` (empty array = old behaviour); detection is the one allowed regex, set before the first parse, cleared when a project without `\s5` opens. `ba309ba`, `5e8a997`
